# Independent Work and Integration

Use a specialist when independent context, evidence gathering, or conclusions
can improve the decision, reveal anchoring, or advance a bounded slice. Parallel
work is useful when scopes and dependencies allow it. An extra context improves
the result only when it brings something the parent or another reader lacks:
fresh eyes, a specialist question, or separable work. Readers that repeat one
reading or split a chain that one reader should follow produce overlapping,
fragmented findings. Perspectives are questions, not headcount; one specialist
can carry several. There is no default worker quota or mandatory delegation
stage for ordinary work. Explicit deep audits follow Audit's coverage and fresh
independent synthesis requirements.

Pass the goal, scope, settled decisions, authorization boundary, acceptance,
and source facts. Label parent interpretations as hypotheses. Reviewers should
inspect evidence independently rather than inherit a verdict. Avoid passing
irrelevant conversation history that anchors their analysis. Independence means
forming a separate judgment, not discarding the user's constraints or the facts
needed to understand the task. Bound each independent slice. Keep bulk output
and long files out of the parent when a summary preserves the decision-relevant
facts. Do not drop settled decisions, authorization, or needed evidence to save
context. For an ambiguous design, an independent reader's
plain-language reconstruction can expose a missing relationship before another
round of recommendations.

Keep read-only roles read-only. Assign a clear owner for each write scope and
protect peer changes. A child cannot obtain more authorization by delegating.
Children do not commit, merge, push, publish, or expand scope unless explicitly
authorized. The parent integrates results, resolves conflicts, and checks the
combined work before claiming delivery. Inspect actual returned evidence: a
failed, cancelled, or unavailable delegate is not a review with zero findings,
and a role label alone does not prove which instructions or permissions ran.

Findings should explain location, consequence, and evidence; ordinary reviews
also identify a needed correction. Initial deep-audit reviewers withhold repair
proposals until fresh independent synthesis. Collect related findings before
repairing their shared root. Communicate unknowns and material unexamined
surfaces in the format useful to the recipient.
